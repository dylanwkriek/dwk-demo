## Page 3 — Continuing the work: monthly retainer, November 2026 to March 2027

The foundation is built. What turns it into jobs is steady, consistent work every month: keeping the system running, producing content and following up every lead. A retainer from now until we reconvene in March 2027 covers exactly that, at a fixed and predictable cost.

**Recommended plan**
| Phase | Package | Monthly | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now, until the public launch | **Premium** | **R999** | Hosting, maintenance, updates, security and backups, new features, adding job photos, quote-form and WhatsApp checks, Privacy/PAIA upkeep including the Information Regulator ticket |
| From launch to March 2027 | **Deluxe** | **R1,500** | Everything in Premium, plus marketing and lead generation (below) |

*Advertising spend (Meta/TikTok) is separate, paid directly by GoodLife, and only with your approval. GoodLife owns all its content and business assets.*

**What Deluxe adds for GoodLife**
- Social content: up to 12 posts a month on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, including 4 short videos of real GoodLife jobs
- Google Business Profile: services, towns, hours and photos kept current, weekly posts, and reviews and questions answered
- Review requests: a short message to each finished customer asking for a review
- Local SEO: service-and-town pages from Hibberdene to Port Edward, consistent listings and an updated project gallery
- Managed advertising: testing the three homeowner ad angles, targeted only at the service area
- Lead tracking: every enquiry logged, a reminder to quote within 24 hours, three follow-ups, and a one-page monthly report (enquiries, quotes, jobs won, best source)
- Business pipeline: working through the 152 prospects (hotels, estates, body corporates, retirement villages), contacted lawfully

**Content that suits GoodLife** (all proof content uses real GoodLife jobs; nothing is faked)
1. Before and after: "Same house. Two weeks apart."
2. Built for South Coast weather: wind, rain and shade tests
3. Salt-air durability: why coastal homes need the right materials
4. Suburb spotlights: a finished job each week in Uvongo, Ramsgate, Hibberdene and other service towns
5. Quote in 24 hours: measure on site, then the quote on WhatsApp the same day *(only if GoodLife can keep that promise)*
6. Meet the team: the owners and crew at work, and how it's done properly
7. Summer shade and paving makeovers: "Get your shade up before the holidays"

*All marketing follows POPIA section 69: no unsolicited electronic marketing without consent, and every message identifies the sender and offers an opt-out.*
