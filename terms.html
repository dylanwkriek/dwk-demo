Service agreement.

This is the plain-English agreement for our website and marketing packages. It covers what you get, how you pay, how you can cancel, and who owns your content.

Last updated November 2026.

Packages

Premium, R999 a month

Premium includes:

  • Hosting
  • Maintenance
  • Updates
  • New features
  • Adding your business photos

Deluxe, R1,500 a month

Deluxe includes everything in Premium, plus:

  • Up to 12 social media posts a month across Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, up to 4 of them short videos
  • SEO for your website and Google Business Profile
  • Managed paid ads
  • A simple monthly report

Billing

You pay monthly, in advance.

Cancellation

You may cancel anytime. Give us 30 days' written notice. A WhatsApp message or an email counts. There is no long-term lock-in. The service runs to the end of the month you have already paid for.

Your content

You own your content and business assets. That includes your logo, photos, text, business information, and social accounts. If you cancel, we hand these back when you ask.
